The Story

Why Threat Hunter's exists

Phishing links, scam emails, and infected files are some of the most common ways people get hacked — and most of it could be avoided with a 5-second check before clicking. Threat Hunter's was built to make that check fast, free, and easy for anyone.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a quick second opinion before opening a link, downloading a file, or trusting an email — no security background needed.

The approach

Instead of relying on a single black-box score, Threat Hunter's runs a set of transparent, rule-based checks and shows you exactly which ones passed or failed — so you understand why something looks risky, not just that it does.

FAQ

Is this 100% accurate?

No — Threat Hunter's uses rule-based checks, not a full security engine. Always verify suspicious links manually and don't rely on it as your only line of defense.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser — nothing you scan is sent to a server or saved.

What's coming next?

Email header analysis and file signature scanning are actively in progress, along with more advanced detection rules for the URL scanner.

Is this a company or a personal project?

Threat Hunter's is an independent, personal project — built to learn web development and cybersecurity fundamentals, and to give people a genuinely useful free tool.

⚠

Threat Hunter's is a learning project and rule-based scanner, not a replacement for professional security software. When in doubt about a link, email, or file — don't click, and verify through an official channel.
